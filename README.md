## 🌱 What is Ava?

Ava is a friendly, **fully-autonomous AI agent** that invests, rebalances, and explains — for the 100M Americans who want to invest but don't know where to start.

Instead of charts and jargon, your portfolio is a **literal coin tower** — visible, satisfying, and honest. The AI handles every trade across stocks and mutual funds. You just watch the tower grow.

## ✨ Core Features

- 🪙 **Coin tower portfolio** — 1 coin = $250. Drop money in, coins stack up. Cash out, they fly away.
- 🤖 **Fully autonomous AI agent** — Trades and rebalances across stocks & mutual funds on its own.
- 💬 **Plain-English transparency** — Every move explained: what, why, what it cost, how it fits your goal.
- 🔮 **Scenario-driven rebalancing** — *"What if the market crashes 20%?"* Rehearse the future without touching real money.
- 🌦️ **Reactive market modes** — Sunny, rainy, storm, market-closed, inflation. Backgrounds, animations, and Ava's voice all adapt.
- 🎯 **Goal-based saving** — Pick what you're saving for (house, education, freedom fund, etc.) and watch progress with confetti at 25%, 50%, and 100%.
- 🔥 **Streaks + tower levels** — From 🌱 *Just Sprouting* to 🗼 *Lighthouse*. Gamified, celebratory, never overwhelming.
- 🔁 **Auto-invest** — Like a savings habit. Set it once, forget it.
